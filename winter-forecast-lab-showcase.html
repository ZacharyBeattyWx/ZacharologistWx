The core idea

Every proposed forecasting relationship is a testable hypothesis. The goal is not merely to produce a winter outlook, but to discover whether an atmospheric signal has repeatable predictive value on previously unseen winters.

Backtesting: predicting winters not seen during training

Train on earlier winters, predict a withheld later winter, measure the error, then advance the window. This reduces the risk of future information leaking into the prediction.

What is the baseline?

Climatology — a simple historical reference that a more complex prediction should improve upon.

A meaningful negative result

One experimental ridge approach underperformed climatology on tested targets. The evaluation process is meant to expose this, rather than inflate claims of success.
